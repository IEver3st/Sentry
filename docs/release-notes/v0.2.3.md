This PC can now scan multiple drives and folders into one storage map.

- Choose locations from the new Drives & folders picker, including a Select all drives option.
- Move between the combined map and individual locations without rescanning.
- Keep selections and the last completed map across launches. Existing single-folder settings and cached scans still work.
- Count overlapping folders once and avoid repeating disk capacity for folders on the same volume.
- Show unavailable locations when part of a scan fails. Cancelling a scan preserves the previous saved result.
- Protect selected drive and folder roots from Recycle Bin actions.

Includes the taskbar and notification branding fixes from earlier releases. The Windows x64 installer is unsigned.
